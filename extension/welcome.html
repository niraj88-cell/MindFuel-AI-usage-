Before anything, here's exactly what this extension sees.

Chrome just asked you to allow "reading your browsing history." That prompt sounds broad, so here is the honest, complete boundary of what SatyaShift actually does.

What it reads

  • ✓ The bare domain of your active tab — for example github.com, never the full address.
  • ✓ How long that domain stays focused, so your focus time can be verified.

What it never sees or does

  • ✗ The page path, the URL after the domain, or anything you type.
  • ✗ Page content, forms, passwords, or your history of other tabs.
  • ✗ Banking, health, email, and password-manager sites — those are skipped entirely.
  • ✗ It never blocks, redirects, or slows down any page.
Why it needs tab access: to know which site currently has your attention, the browser has to tell the extension the active tab. SatyaShift immediately reduces that to the domain and throws the rest away. The domain stays private to your account — your squad never sees it, not even the one you focused on.
Open SatyaShift

You can pause tracking anytime from the toolbar icon. Everything is stored on your account and yours to delete.
